DOUGLAS privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Who uses it

DOUGLAS is a personal assistant that Sawyer Valin runs on his own computer for himself. It isn't a public service, and the only Google accounts it connects to are Sawyer's own.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: it reads, searches, labels and archives messages, and sends mail only when Sawyer approves each message.
  • Google Calendar: it reads events and creates, updates or deletes them on Sawyer's request.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to carry out what Sawyer asks, for example summarizing his inbox, tracking his subscriptions and bills, or managing his calendar.
  • Sign-in tokens and any notes DOUGLAS keeps are stored on Sawyer's computer and nowhere else.
  • To answer a request, the relevant text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, that data isn't used to train their models.
  • Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Google API Services

DOUGLAS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops DOUGLAS from reading anything further.
